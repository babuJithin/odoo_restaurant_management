Restaurant Food Ordering Application

Our restaurant food ordering application is designed to streamline operations for restaurants that serve customers through online orders. This solution offers a user-friendly interface for both restaurant staff and customers, enabling efficient order management, tracking, and delivery. Integrated seamlessly with Odoo's powerful backend, the application provides all the tools needed for running a successful food ordering system, from menu management to payment processing.

Features Highlight

Customizable UI/UX

A simple, intuitive interface for both customers and staff, ensuring a smooth experience from food selection to order completion.

User Registration & Login

Customers can create an account and log in using email or social credentials to personalize their experience and track order history.

Menu Management

A comprehensive system for restaurant owners to manage and update their menu with categories, descriptions, prices, and images of dishes.

Online Ordering

Customers can browse the menu, select dishes, customize orders (e.g., choosing portion sizes, add-ons), and place an order seamlessly.

Order Tracking

Real-time tracking allows customers to follow the status of their orders from preparation to delivery or pickup.

Inventory Management

A system to keep track of ingredient stock levels, preventing orders of items that are out of stock and ensuring kitchen efficiency.

Push Notifications & Alerts

Automatic notifications are sent to customers regarding order confirmation, preparation updates, delivery status, and promotional messages.

Delivery & Pickup Options

The app supports both delivery and in-store pickup, giving customers the flexibility to choose their preferred method when placing an order.

Delivery Fee Management

The system calculates delivery fees based on customer location, ensuring correct charges and transparent pricing.

Order History & Reordering

Customers can view their past orders and quickly reorder their favorite dishes with one click, enhancing repeat business.

Admin Dashboard

A backend interface for the restaurant owner or admin to monitor orders, manage menus, track inventory, and generate sales reports.

Customer Feedback System

Allows customers to rate their experience and leave feedback on individual dishes, enabling the restaurant to improve quality.
